Customer churn prediction with a profit-based threshold

scikit-learnExplainabilityFastAPIPydanticDockerpandas
+70%
expected retention profit vs contacting every customer

Predicts which telecom customers are likely to leave and decides who should get a retention offer. The decision threshold maximises expected profit, using the cost of an offer and the revenue it can save, instead of plain accuracy. Results are measured on a simulated dataset that uses the column layout of the public IBM Telco churn data.
